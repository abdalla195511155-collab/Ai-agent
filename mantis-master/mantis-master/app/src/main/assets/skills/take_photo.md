# Skill: Take Photo

## Trigger
take a photo, take a picture, photograph, capture, selfie, camera, shoot, snap

## Steps
1. Open the Camera app from the home screen or app drawer.
2. Wait for the camera viewfinder to load — the live preview should be visible.
3. If the user asked for a selfie or front-facing photo, tap the camera flip/rotate icon to switch to the front camera.
4. If the user specified a mode (video, portrait, panorama), tap the corresponding mode tab at the top or bottom of the viewfinder.
5. Aim the camera (the agent cannot physically move the phone — proceed to capture).
6. Tap the shutter button (large white/colored circle, usually at the bottom center) to take the photo.
7. Wait for the capture animation to complete and the photo to be saved.
8. Call complete.

## Notes
- The shutter button is the large circular button at the bottom center of the viewfinder — do not confuse it with the gallery thumbnail or flip-camera button.
- On Samsung, the front/rear flip icon is in the bottom-right area of the viewfinder.
- If the Camera app requests permissions (camera, microphone, storage), tap "Allow" for each.
- If Camera was already open in another mode, do not change the mode unless the user explicitly asked for one.
- The agent cannot physically orient the phone — if the user asks to "point at X", capture immediately without trying to describe aiming.
