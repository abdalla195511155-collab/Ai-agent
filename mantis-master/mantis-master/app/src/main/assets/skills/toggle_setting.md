# Skill: Toggle Setting

## Trigger
turn on, turn off, enable, disable, toggle, switch on, switch off, activate, deactivate, dark mode, light mode, do not disturb, airplane mode, bluetooth, mobile data, hotspot, auto-rotate, location, sound, vibrate, silent, night mode

## Steps
1. Open the Settings app from the home screen, app drawer, or by pulling down the notification shade and tapping the gear icon.
2. Use the search bar at the top of Settings (magnifying glass icon) and type the setting name (e.g. "dark mode", "bluetooth", "do not disturb").
3. Tap the most relevant search result to navigate directly to the setting.
4. Find the toggle switch for the target setting. Tap it to turn it ON or OFF as requested.
5. Confirm the toggle is now in the correct state (ON = filled/colored, OFF = gray/empty).
6. Call complete.

## Notes
- Quick Settings shortcuts: many toggles (WiFi, Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, Airplane Mode, Dark Mode) are accessible by pulling down the notification shade twice — this is faster than opening full Settings.
- Dark mode on Samsung: Settings → Display → Dark mode, or notification shade toggle.
- Do Not Disturb on Samsung: Settings → Sounds and vibration → Do not disturb.
- If the search returns no results, navigate manually: pull down the notification shade and look for the toggle in Quick Settings tiles.
- After tapping a toggle, wait one step for the UI to reflect the change before confirming.
