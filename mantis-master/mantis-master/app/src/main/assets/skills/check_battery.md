# Skill: Check Battery

## Trigger
battery, battery level, how much battery, charge, charging status, battery percentage, power, battery health, battery usage, battery saver

## Steps
1. Pull down the notification shade by swiping down from the top of the screen.
2. The battery percentage is typically shown in the top-right status bar area or in the quick settings panel.
3. If the percentage is not visible in the shade, open the Settings app.
4. Tap the search icon and type "battery".
5. Tap "Battery" or "Battery and device care" from the results.
6. Read the battery percentage and charging status shown at the top of the battery settings screen.
7. If the user asked for battery usage by app, scroll down to the "Battery usage" section on this screen.
8. Report what you found in the "complete" description, then call complete.

## Notes
- Samsung OneUI: Settings → Battery and device care → Battery. The main screen shows the percentage prominently.
- The status bar always shows a battery icon — the percentage may need to be enabled in Settings → Notifications → Status bar → Show battery percentage.
- "Battery saver" or "Power saving mode" toggle is on the Battery settings screen.
- If the user asks to ENABLE battery saver (not just check), tap the "Power saving" toggle on the Battery page and confirm it is ON.
- Charging status: if the phone is plugged in, the battery icon shows a lightning bolt and the screen may say "Charging".
